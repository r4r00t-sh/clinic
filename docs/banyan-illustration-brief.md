# Illustration brief — Banyan signature art (fallback)

Procedural SVG is shipping as a placeholder. Commission refined artwork when budget allows.

## Deliverables

Three layered SVG illustrations:

1. **Hero canopy** — top-heavy, croppable off top and sides; hanging aerial roots along left/right
2. **Full scene tree** — complete Ficus benghalensis from soil to flat canopy (~900–1100px wide at 1440)
3. **Footer roots** — roots only along a soil line; should tile horizontally

Each file: named groups `#canopy-back`, `#canopy-mid`, `#branches`, `#roots`, `#trunk`, `#ground`. Clean paths, no rasters, under 120KB each.

## Style

Refined botanical line-and-flat-fill (engraving meets calm flat illustration).

- Linework 1–1.25px in `#6F7D3C` (olive-deep) at 55–65% opacity
- Flat fills in olive at ~18%, 28%, 40% opacity
- No outlines thicker than 1.5px
- No gradients except soft turmeric light rays at 6–10%
- Aerial roots / trunk: bark brown (ink at 35–55%) with a touch of clay
- Soil: sand

## Botanical features

Very wide flat-topped canopy; thick gnarled buttressed trunk; many thin hanging aerial roots, some thickening into pillar trunks; broad ovate leaves with slightly heart-shaped base and pointed tip, clustered organically (not uniform).

## Usage notes

Hero text must sit on clear ground under the canopy. Scene tree hosts four HTML callouts on branches. Footer roots stay decorative at low opacity over olive-deep footer.
